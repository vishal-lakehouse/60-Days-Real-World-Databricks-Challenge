# 📖 Business Requirement

Olist Technologies has started a Retail Lakehouse Modernization Project.

The company currently receives large amounts of customer orders, seller information, product details, reviews, and payment transactions every day.

Before the Data Engineering team begins building data pipelines, every engineer must prepare a standardized development environment.

Without a common environment:

- Different developers use different settings.
- Code becomes difficult to maintain.
- Collaboration becomes inefficient.
- Production deployments become risky.

Your Tech Lead has assigned your first Jira Story:

**Prepare the Databricks development environment.**
