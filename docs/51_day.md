# 🏢 Business Scenario

**Client Name:** Olist Marketplace

Olist plans to modernize its legacy reporting platform.

Current challenges include:

- Reports take several hours to refresh.
- Multiple departments maintain duplicate datasets.
- Business users lack trusted KPIs.
- Manual ETL processes frequently fail.
- Data quality issues delay decision-making.
- The existing reporting platform cannot scale with business growth.

The executive leadership team has hired your consulting company to design and deliver a modern Azure Databricks Lakehouse solution.

Today marks the official **Project Kick-Off Meeting**.

Your responsibility is to understand the client's business requirements before writing a single line of code.
